Read it as the product's thesis, demonstrated on itself: a death is noticed and its cause graded before anything moves; the chain is checked before anything resumes; a refusal is spoken aloud and receipted, never papered over. The watch does not guess, and it does not stay silent about what it cannot prove.

The stall sentences recur by design on this run: the instrument signs a checkpoint every ~505 s against the supervisor's 180 s stale threshold, so a healthy watch earns stall receipts on a cadence sawtooth — diagnosed background, filed against RUN-9 as RUN-9's DIAGNOSIS-STALL-ANOMALY.md.
